== Frequently asked questions

=== Is Nod.ie public and open source?
Yes. The repository is https://github.com/KnowAll-AI/Nod.ie and the root LICENSE contains the MIT terms.

=== Does scanning use the GPU?
No. Scans inspect metadata and query releases; they do not invoke language or speech models.

=== Does Nod.ie update containers automatically?
No. Scanning is automatic while the desktop runs; applying an update requires a reviewed plan and native desktop confirmation. Some deployments require their official update tool.

=== Is ElevenLabs required?
No. There is no ElevenLabs integration in this implementation. Local mode uses existing local transcription, Ollama and WAV speech services. Adding a paid/cloud provider is a separate integration decision.

=== Does Nod.ie float over the desktop or run on the lock screen?

The Electron interface is a transparent, frameless, always-on-top desktop window. Click and drag Nod.ie herself to move her, or use the optional orange/amber handle above her. A short click starts or stops the selected voice interaction; dragging does not trigger it. The browser is an alternative interface. KDE's protected lock screen does not display ordinary desktop windows above it. Lock-screen voice interaction is not implemented; it would need a separate, restricted KDE integration. Do not rely on screen locking alone to cancel an in-progress voice turn in this release; stop the turn before locking.

=== Does she have neural lip sync?

Yes, when the optional local MuseTalk service is configured. It generates her mouth movements from each speech reply and plays the video and audio together. Rendering adds a short delay before playback. If the service is unavailable, speech still plays with a visible lip-sync warning.

=== What do the buttons around Nod.ie do?

The lower-left Lucide microphone button starts recording; clicking again sends the recording. Clicking it while she is replying interrupts that reply and starts a new recording. The lower-right speaker button mutes or unmutes actual audio output, including neural speech video; the choice survives a reload on that interface. These controls are separate from the avatar drag surface.

The bottom camera button is visibly off and unavailable because camera capture and vision are not yet connected. Clicking it explains this without requesting camera permission. Llama 3.1 8B is text-only. Family face recognition and speaker identification require separate integration and opt-in enrolment; neither is active in this release.

The avatar is quiet while idle. Routine status indicators use brief state names such as "Listening", "Thinking" and "Speaking", without persistent "click to" instructions.

=== Can Nod.ie look up our Bitcoin version, channel count and liquidity herself?

Yes, in local voice mode she can choose the read-only `get_node_status` tool, including for follow-up questions. It queries the local `bitcoind` and `lnd` containers using fixed CLI commands and the LND read-only macaroon. She receives fresh versions, channel counts, balances and reserves and chooses her own wording. Missing access is reported as unavailable; she cannot make payments or run arbitrary shell commands. Balances are not a guarantee that a payment can be routed.

=== Can I put her over the system clock?

Yes. The desktop overlay can cross the panel boundary and partially leave the screen, with a recoverable portion kept visible. Linux uses a dock-type window to prevent the panel avoidance that previously caused upward drift.
